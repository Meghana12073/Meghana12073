## 👋 About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate (B.E., 2026 · CGPA 8.63) from Bengaluru, building my career around one question: **what is this data actually telling us to do?**

Across 4 end-to-end analytics projects in retail, telecom, and supply chain, I've consistently found the same pattern — a small, specific slice of the business (one supplier tier, one customer segment, one region) is quietly responsible for most of the outcome. Finding that slice, proving it with data, and packaging it into something a stakeholder can act on in five minutes is what I care about most.

```text
if you give me a raw dataset  → I'll give you back a root cause, a number, and a dashboard.
```

- 🔎 **Root-cause first** — I don't stop at "churn is up," I dig until I can say *why*, and *how much*
- 📊 **Dashboard-native** — every project ships with a working BI dashboard, not just a notebook
- 🏆 **3rd Prize**, IRJCS International Paper Conference (2025) — out of 80+ papers — real-time data pipelines & IoT analytics
- 💼 Former **Software Developer Intern** (Full Stack) @ SuprMentr Technologies — shipped defect-rate KPI reporting that cut post-release defects ~30%
- 🎯 Currently open to **Data Analyst / Business Analyst / Product Analyst** roles
